Frequently Asked Questions for B2B Sourcing Managers

Q1: What is the benefit of your hand-stitched volleyballs compared to machine-stitched ones?

Our hand-stitched volleyballs are crafted by expert technicians using thicker, pre-stretched polyester threads. The seams are pulled much tighter and deeper into the panels, which prevents water absorption, ensures a softer tactile touch, and protects the stitches from premature wear on rough courts. Machine-stitched balls, while less expensive, are perfect for school training and volume-driven promotional merchandising where price-competitiveness is key.

Q2: How do you guarantee the roundness, bounce, and air retention of your volleyballs?

Every premium ball features a heavy-duty butyl bladder reinforced with a dense nylon winding matrix and multi-layer fabric backing. This premium sandwich construction keeps the ball perfectly spherical under high impact, delivers standard-compliant rebound elasticity, and maintains constant internal air pressure for months without requiring deflation or frequent re-pumping.

Q3: What is the typical MOQ for custom branded volleyballs?

For custom private label volleyballs (sizes 4 and 5), our standard MOQ starts at 500 pieces per design/size. For standard wholesale items or promotional corporate gifts, we can accommodate lower quantities for your initial trial order. Contact our sales team for an immediate direct-factory assessment.
