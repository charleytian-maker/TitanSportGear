We collect personal data only when you submit an inquiry form, including your name, email address, phone number, company name and product requirements. We also use Google Analytics to collect anonymous website browsing data for improving user experience.

You have the right to request access, correction, deletion of your personal data, and to withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us via info@titansportgear.com.
