Frequently Asked Questions for B2B Sourcing Managers

Q1: What makes your natural rubber playground balls ideal for institutional school use?

Our playground balls are specifically engineered for high-abuse institutional environments. By combining 100% natural rubber with a dense nylon wound carcass and a premium butyl bladder, our balls withstand heavy kicking, asphalt scuffing, and constant sun exposure without cracking, losing roundness, or leaking air. The classic textured surface ensures a confident grip for children of all ages.

Q2: Do your playground balls pass safety certifications for the US and European markets?

Yes, absolutely. We use premium, eco-friendly natural rubber and non-toxic color pigments. Our products are fully tested and certified to comply with strict safety standards, including US CPSIA (Prop 65) and European REACH regulations (Phthalate-free and heavy-metal-free), making them safe for school and kindergarten environments.

Q3: What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) for custom colors and custom logos?

Our standard MOQ for custom branded rubber playground balls starts at 1,000 pieces per size/color. For standard stock colors with custom logo printing, we can accommodate lower quantities for initial market trial orders. Please contact our export team to discuss your bundle design.
