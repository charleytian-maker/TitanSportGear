Frequently Asked Questions for B2B Sourcing Managers

Q1: What bladder and winding construction do you use to guarantee the ball's shape and air retention?

Every single premium basketball we manufacture is built with a high-grade butyl rubber bladder, which provides vastly superior air-retention compared to standard latex bladders. To ensure the basketball never deforms or wobbles during play, the bladder is wrapped in a dense, high-tensile nylon thread winding matrix. This structural integrity guarantees a perfect bounce and roundness over years of heavy use.

Q2: What is the benefit of your wet-processed (microfiber) composite leather basketballs?

Our wet-processed composite leather (commonly known in the industry as wet-processed microfiber) provides an ultra-soft, premium tactile hand-feel that closely mimics genuine leather but with advanced moisture-wicking and sweat-absorbent capabilities. It gives players a non-slip grip right out of the box without requiring a "break-in" period, making it a top seller for premium tournament lines and Amazon storefronts.

Q3: Do your basketballs undergo testing before shipping from the factory?

Yes, quality assurance is our top priority. Every basketball undergoes a mandatory 24-hour inflation and air-tightness leak test, alongside weight and sphericity calibration checks. We ensure that 100% of the products delivered to your warehouse meet strict professional weight and bounce standards.
