Frequently Asked Questions for B2B Sourcing Managers

Q1: What are the main benefits of Polyester vs. Polypropylene (PP) for commercial sports nets?

Polyester offers incredible tensile strength and low elasticity, making it perfect for nets that require constant high tension (like badminton and volleyball) or indoor environments where shape-retention is key. Polypropylene (PP), especially high-density braided PP, is outstanding for heavy-impact outdoor use (like soccer goal nets). PP absorbs virtually no water, float on water, and has excellent chemical resistance against mildew, mold, and harsh outdoor elements.

Q2: Can you supply complete portable net systems with adjustable height frames for school use?

The telescopic posts can be adjusted to official heights for pickleball, tennis, badminton, or youth volleyball, making them an incredibly versatile, high-margin product for school contracts and consumer retail brands.
