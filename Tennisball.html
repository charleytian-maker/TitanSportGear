Frequently Asked Questions for B2B Buyers

Q1: What materials are used in your tennis balls to ensure durability and good bounce?

Our tennis balls are engineered using a high-purity natural rubber inner core, which provides permanent internal pressure and consistent elasticity. The outer shell is wrapped in tightly woven needle felt with high wool content, ensuring excellent aerodynamics, optimal grip on the racket strings, and maximum durability on both clay and hard courts.

Q2: Can we order custom tennis racket sets with mixed specifications for retail?

Yes, absolutely. We specialize in OEM tennis bundle customization. You can customize a full retail set containing a mix of aluminum rackets for beginners, full carbon rackets for intermediate players, a pack of custom felt balls, and replacement sweat-absorbent overgrips, all packed inside a carrying bag with your brand logo.

Q3: What is the typical MOQ for custom branded tennis rackets and balls?

For custom logo printed tennis rackets (aluminum or carbon), our standard MOQ starts at 300 pieces per model. For custom branded tennis balls, the MOQ starts at 1,000-3,000 pieces depending on the packaging type (cans or mesh bags). We offer flexible support for your first trial order.
