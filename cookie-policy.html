Cookie Policy

Our website uses cookies to ensure basic functionality and improve your browsing experience. We only use two types of cookies:

1. Necessary cookies: Required for website navigation, form submission, cannot be disabled.

2. Analytical cookies: Anonymous data collected by Google Analytics to analyze website traffic and optimize page structure.

You can accept or reject non-essential cookies through the cookie consent banner on our homepage. Rejecting analytical cookies will not affect your normal use of the website.
